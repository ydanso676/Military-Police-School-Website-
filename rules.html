OFFICIAL REGULATIONS

SCHOOL RULES

Standards of conduct, discipline, and professionalism for all Military Police School personnel.

RULES & REGULATIONS

GENERAL SCHOOL POLICIES

01

Professional Conduct

  • Treat all personnel with respect.
  • Harassment, discrimination, and bullying are prohibited.
  • Maintain professional behavior at all times.
  • Respect the established chain of command.
02

Training Regulations

  • Follow instructions from authorized instructors.
  • Remain attentive during training.
  • Do not disrupt or interfere with sessions.
  • Notify your instructor before leaving training.
03

Uniform & Appearance

  • Wear the designated uniform when required.
  • Follow the established dress code.
  • Do not wear unauthorized accessories during official training.
04

Instructor Responsibilities

  • Remain professional and impartial.
  • Never abuse instructor authority.
  • Treat all trainees fairly.
  • Provide clear instructions and feedback.
  • Report serious violations to leadership.
05

Communication

  • Use appropriate language.
  • Do not spam official channels.
  • Keep discussions relevant to their channels.
  • Do not share confidential information without authorization.
06

Academic Integrity

  • Cheating during examinations is prohibited.
  • Do not distribute examination answers.
  • Do not falsify attendance or training records.
  • Plagiarism and unauthorized assistance are prohibited.
07

Authority & Discipline

  • Do not misuse rank or school authority.
  • Follow legitimate instructions from school leadership.
  • Report misconduct through the chain of command.
  • Violations may result in disciplinary action.
08

General Regulations

  • Follow all applicable FUSM regulations.
  • Do not impersonate instructors or leadership.
  • Do not exploit glitches or use unauthorized tools.
  • Cooperate with official inspections and evaluations.

IMPORTANT NOTICE

Failure to follow these regulations may result in a warning, removal from training, suspension, or dismissal from the Military Police School, depending on the severity of the violation.
