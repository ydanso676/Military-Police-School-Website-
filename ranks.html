RANK STRUCTURE

Explore the command structure and instructor ranks of the Military Police School.

SCHOOL COMMAND

HIGH COMMAND

0-10

Chairman of Military Police School

The Chairman runs Military Police School and represents it, builds and grows the division, and oversees the other members of High Command.

Be selected by MP Leadership and approved by Military Police Overseer.

0-9

Vice Chairman of Military Police School

Assists the Chairman and supports the administration of the school.

0-8

Senior Enlisted Advisor

Provides enlisted leadership guidance and supports school standards.

INSTRUCTOR COMMAND

MIDDLE COMMAND

APPOINTED

Aide-De-Camp

Supports the senior leadership of the Military Police School.

0-7

Chief Instructor

Oversees instructor operations and training standards.

0-6

Head Instructor

Supervises instructors and supports the delivery of school courses.

LOW COMMAND

0-5

Lead Instructor

Assists with instructor coordination and training quality.

0-4

Senior Instructor

Demonstrates experience and assists with instruction.

0-3

Instructor

Conducts assigned instruction and evaluates trainees.

0-2

Junior Instructor

Develops instructional ability under the guidance of senior personnel.

0-1

Instructor In Training

Completes instructor development and demonstrates readiness for the role.
