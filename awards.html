MPS RECOGNITION SYSTEM

MEDALS & AWARDS

Official Military Police School departmental decorations and recognition.

OFFICIAL AWARD CATALOG

Fifteen departmental awards recognizing excellence, service, and achievement.

MPS-001

Defense Meritorious Service Medal

Performing exceptionally well to the point where HQ are not required to intervene or consistently keep telling you issues that you should be identifying and ensuring your overall division is strong and up to date [Awarded to E9B+, holding a leadership position of higher responsibility on a level higher than the Meritorious Service Medal.]

MPS-002

Meritorious Service Medal

Given to individuals who are after 1+ months of putting in the effort and dedication to learn and improve within the division, managing and working alongside individuals below them are working to exceptional activity and making sure the quality in the positions represent that of HICOM. Awarded non-combative meritorious service to the FUSM. [0-1+ , served for at least 1 month as HICOM for said division.

MPS-003

Instructor Excellence Medal

Exceptional instruction and cadet development.

MPS-004

Sliver Star Medal

Same ways as Bronze except the CT KDR greater than 1.5, for F vs F or deployments required to show EXCEPTIONAL callouts & 4+ kills, during FM raids to kill FM at least 20 times regardless of KDR

MPS-005

Outstanding Service Medal

Exceptional departmental service.

MPS-006

Meritorious Achievement Medal

Significant achievements benefiting MPS.

MPS-007

Bronze Star Medal

This can be given from MPS HICOM CT's, Force v Force or Deployments. You are required to perform average in Kills to Deaths, meaning receive a KDR in CTs greater than 0.7 to achieve. Within Force vs Force/Deployments, you are required to give mediocre communication on enemy wereabouts or achieve 1 or 2 kills. Medal can be received from raids as well killing raiders at least 10 times regardless of kill to death ratio.

MPS-008

Joint Service Commendation Medal

Taken part in a minimum of 5 events with allies leading said event with no complications. Awarded to O-5 individuals for displaying a distinguished achievement or service when participating in an allied event.

MPS-009

Joint Service Achievement Medal

This is taken part in a minimum of 3 events with allies and assisting those leading to ensure the event runs smoothly, this can be an event in or out of MA Awarded to individuals of the paygrade of O6 (Colonel) or below for making incredible contributions for their division during a joint duty, both in a combative and non-combative setting.

MPS-010

Exemplary Conduct Medal

Exceptional integrity and professionalism.

MPS-011

Discipline Medal

Outstanding discipline and adherence to standards.

MPS-012

Good Conduct Medal

This can be given to individuals who has been actively putting their dedication into their said subdivision. Awarded for honorable and meritorious service outside of combat zone.

MPS-013

Service Recognition Medal

Distinguished service and departmental dedication.

MPS-014

Training Excellence Medal

Outstanding performance during training.

MPS-015

Special Achievement Medal

Exceptional accomplishments deserving special recognition.
