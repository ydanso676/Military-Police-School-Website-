WHO WE ARE

About the School

Our Mission

The FUSM Military Police School prepares personnel to understand department standards, responsibilities, procedures, and expectations. Our goal is to develop capable and dependable members through organized instruction and fair evaluation.

Our Standards

All students are expected to demonstrate respect, maturity, communication, integrity, and willingness to learn. Instructors are expected to teach clearly, apply standards consistently, and lead by example.
