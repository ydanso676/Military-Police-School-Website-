ABOUT THE SCHOOL

The Military Police School is responsible for recruiting, educating, and developing personnel to meet the standards of the Military Police. Through structured instruction and professional development, trainees are prepared for their responsibilities.

01

DISCIPLINE

Maintaining high standards of conduct, responsibility, and professionalism.

02

INTEGRITY

Promoting honesty, fairness, and accountability throughout the school.

03

LEADERSHIP

Preparing personnel to lead, instruct, and support their fellow members.
